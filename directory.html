FS Animation Directory

These product animations are in Lottie format. Lottie files are very efficient, but they're stored in JSON format, so you can't really download and play them locally. This page gives you a few options.

  • View animations in your browser by clicking on the title, or quickly copy the link with .
  • Notion supports Lottie natively – just copy the relevant link with , then use "/embed" in Notion and paste the link.
  • For Powerpoint or direct sharing, you can view and download any animation in .mp4 format with .
  • Copy the JSON link with - for embedding in other contexts (i.e. Squarespace code blocks).
