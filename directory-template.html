FS Animation Directory

These animations are in Lottie format. Lottie files are really efficient, but fiddly to use – they're stored in JSON format, so you can't really download and play them locally. This site gives you a few different options:

  • View animations in your browser by clicking on the title, or quickly copy the link with .
  • Notion supports Lottie natively – just copy the relevant link with , then use "/embed" in Notion and paste the link.
  • For other contexts like Powerpoint, or if you want to share the file directly, you can view and download any animation in .mp4 format with .
  • Disregard unless you need the direct JSON link.
